Delete your CareBridge account

Enter the email address on the account. We will send you a link to confirm — that link is what proves the account is yours, since we cannot ask you to sign in here.

Deleting this account will:

  • sign out every device straight away;
  • schedule the account for deletion 30 days from now — signing in again before then cancels it;
  • remove your name, address, phone number, sign-in details and notification history when it runs.

Some records are kept. Invoices, payments and the log of actions taken on the account are retained for as long as financial and audit rules require — with nothing left on them that identifies you. We would rather say so now than have you find out later.

CareBridge coordinates medical appointments and transport. It is not a medical service and not for emergencies.

You can also do this inside the app: Settings → Account security → Delete account.
